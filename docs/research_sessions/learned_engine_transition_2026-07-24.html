Harmonia · research screen · 2026-07-24 · real-audio, cache-only

Can a small learned root+quality engine beat musx — or recover the ±P5 confusion?

A screen of the human’s ingredients — root/bass normalization, an intensity-normalized per-frame delta channel, and previous-chord conditioning — baked into a learned, transposition-invariant engine, trained on POP909 real-audio NNLS chroma and tested against the shipped musx-argmax source on the 7 frozen brick-0 songs.

Verdict — outcome (c): valid null

The learned engine does not beat musx (−9.0pp root, loses on 6 of 7 songs), the delta channel adds no marginal value (it slightly hurts every metric), and it recovers only ~12% of the ±P5 segments while damaging ~16% of the ones musx got right. There is no live lever here. The head is competent in-domain (0.90 root on POP909) and collapses 25pp on real audio — so the barrier is the domain gap, and the fifth confusion needs a genuinely different front-end, not features on this chroma.

07Method, honesty & what a win would have hooked

Design. Common “where” grid = music-x-lab’s own spans, so every engine is judged on identity alone. Head A = a learned cascade (root head on the absolute clip-pooled 24-d NNLS chroma; quality head cascade-rotated by the predicted root — the shipped NNLS24Heads topology, retrained on the leakage-robust clip-pool). The delta is the human’s intensity-normalized (unit-L1, energy-gated) directional onset difference, concatenated as one auxiliary channel and ablated on/off. A transition factor and a weak diatonic key tie-break were composed in a root-level Viterbi.

Honesty carried.

  • POP909 = functional root + MIDI-synth lineage; the frozen set is real audio — a real domain gap, and it is the whole story (0.90 → 0.65).
  • Every headline is 5-seed (Head A) / 3-seed (in-domain); std ≤ 0.014 root. musx baseline is deterministic and reproduces the shipped 0.7367.
  • The transition-Viterbi lift is real but is self-transition smoothing, still sub-musx, and uses the fifth-motion prior flagged as risky — reported, not shipped.
  • Fully cache-only (41 POP909 + 7 frozen NNLS/musx caches) — no audio decode, no VAMP, no beat tracking; disk-safe under the concurrent frozen-set agent.

No brick to ship. Nothing beat musx, so there is no default-OFF emission/transition brick to gate. Had Head A won, the hook was an emission swap in FusionChordDecoder (root/quality posterior in place of the musx per-segment label), behind a kill-switch env flag defaulting OFF. The actionable conclusion instead: the ±P5 confusion is acoustic (overtone fifth), so closing it needs a deep audio front-end (BTC-style) trained on real audio — not another pooled-chroma feature, and not the delta.
