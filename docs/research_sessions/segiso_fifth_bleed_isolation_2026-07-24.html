Fifth-bleed: is it segmentation or labeling?

Clean, disk-free isolation on the frozen 7 · 159.7 s of ±P5/P4 root bleed · 2026-07-24. Corrects the beat-snapped oracle screen (which folded boundary error into “labeling”) by sampling music-x-lab’s own raw .lab at the true GT-chord time.

Verdict

The bleed is not dominantly the pipeline’s segmentation. Of 159.7 s, only 19% (30.4 s) is the pipeline sampling the wrong segment (recoverable by re-segmentation). 25% is music-x-lab’s own boundary landing late/early at chord edges, and 56% is music-x-lab predicting the wrong chord even at the true chord center (genuine label error). Louis’s ear is right that it sounds like placement — but the placement error is music-x-lab’s (boundary + label = 81%), not the NNLS segmentation. A grid-decoupled “true-musx-span” re-segmentation recovers exactly the 19% and moves end-to-end root by −0.2 pp (0.7367→0.7347). Fixing segmentation is a null; the lever is musx label/boundary quality.
