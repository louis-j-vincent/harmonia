min→dom label repair — largely NULL, even with voca. Of 30 min→dom errors (musx labels a dominant GT chord as minor), only 8 fall inside firing regions, and voca fixes just 1 (maj/min 0, by vocabulary). The reason is structural: a min-vs-dom mistake is a label error, uncorrelated with the duration-based gate — most min→dom spans are NOT in anomalously-long musx intervals, so a region-gated swap can't reach them. The bake-off's 9/22 is voca's global potential; region-gating is the wrong instrument for it. min→dom needs a label-targeted pass (voca everywhere, or a third/fifth discriminator), not this boundary gate. Global label sanity: musx majmin 0.702 → hybrid 0.703 → voca 0.701 → maj/min 0.699 — no degradation (out-of-region byte-identical by construction).

7 · Integration note & reproduction

Recommendation — the cablable hook (validated end-to-end, §8): keep musx as the boundary+label source globally, and in each FIRING region (local-median-anomalous long musx interval + BTC∩DeepChroma consensus, edge-skip) replace the region with a BTC decode's boundaries AND labels, then stitch → coalesce. This beats the shipped nnls-seg FINAL by +0.040 F1 / +0.073 recall without regressing the clean songs, and the control isolates the differing label as the cause. Exact wiring — the HYBRID: region generator = scratchpad/align_gated_local.py (local-median + edge-skip + BTC∩DeepChroma consensus); region→source swap goes in _infer_nnls24 after segs are built (before _label_segments), replacing in-region segments with BTC-maj/min boundaries labeled by BTC-voca — a region-scoped swap, so out-of-region musx labeling is byte-identical (global root +0.002 / majmin +0.001, no degradation). Best end-to-end: F1 0.706 / R 0.717 vs shipped 0.658 / 0.657. Do NOT expect min→dom label repair from this — those errors sit outside the duration gate (8/30 in-region, 1 fixed); route min→dom to a separate label-targeted pass (voca-everywhere or a third/fifth discriminator). Gate any change on this alignment harness first. (Recall-only, chart-agnostic: musx ∪ DeepChroma h≈0.3–0.4 still gives +0.09 raw-boundary recall for a downstream consumer.)
