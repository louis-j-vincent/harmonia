The chord we keep getting wrong is the dominant

A full inventory of where family-level partial credit is lost on the seven frozen songs, and one default-OFF brick that fixes the single biggest named error in it. Everything below is audible.

1 · What the error actually is

Duration-weighted inventory of every second of partial-credit loss on the seven frozen songs, split by what is wrong with the label rather than by where the boundary sits.

Inside QUALITY_ONLY the confusions are, in seconds: dom→maj 49.3, maj→dom 40.1, min→dom 16.3, min→maj 16.8, hdim→min 13.4, dom→min 10.2. The maj↔dom pair alone is 89.4 s = 5.44% of scoreable time, across 4 of 7 songs.

It beats every root-level function confusion, including i↔V (58.8 s). And unlike i↔V it sits on already-pure slices — 62% and 85% of its time is inside segments that are ≥80% one reference chord. No boundary work can reach it, and none is needed. i↔V is the opposite: 78% of its time is in slices that are less than 80% pure, so it is entangled with segmentation, not a clean identity error.

2 · Two witnesses, neither of which works alone

The acoustic one. music-x-lab's network emits a dedicated 4-way posterior over the seventh degree at 43 fps — literally the one note that turns a I into a V. We never read it. But the vendored Viterbi that writes the label already consumed it, so it is not an independent witness: over 1036 s of 1070 s of maj/dom chart time it simply agrees with what we already print. On its own it is worth +0.78pp at the 0.5 tie and exactly 0.00pp at any real confidence gate.

The context one. Does this root resolve down a perfect fifth to the next chord in the chart? No key model, no fitted parameter.

resolves down a 5threference = domreference = majP(dom)
yes200.2 s54.8 s0.785
no29.8 s383.1 s0.072

Odds lift 47× (51× on the repaired reference), consistent on 6 of 7 songs — the seventh, Every Breath You Take, contains no dominants at all. And yet as a rewrite rule it does not pay: the printed family is already right 86.6% of the time, so overriding it costs −0.83pp one way, buys +1.13pp the other, and swings individual songs by −8 to −18pp.

So the context is used as a weak tie-breaker on the acoustic log-odds, never as a rule. One knob, worth 1.0 nat of swing (≈2.7:1 odds) — deliberately far weaker than the 47× it actually measures. Its only job is to break ties the seventh-degree head cannot.

5 · And here is what a discriminator cannot reach

The mission expected i↔V to be the target. Listen to the two longest ones. The reference and the model do not disagree about the identity of a chord that is sounding — the model is holding one chord straight through a change, so a whole bar of the next harmony is inside a slice named for the previous one. There is no single label that makes that span right, because two different chords are playing in it. That is a boundary, and boundaries are someone else's lane.

6 · What this does not fix

  • Root errors — 62.8% of all lost time — are untouched. mirex_root moves by exactly +0.00pp. That bottleneck is upstream and this brick does not go near it.
  • The root residue is a long tail. 163 distinct (reference → predicted) root-wrong pairs on seven songs; you need 46–56 of them to cover 80% of the time. No targeted discriminator has a shape that fits that.
  • i↔V is not reachable this way — 78% of its time is in impure slices. The hypothesis that the distant residue is a clean function error is only true for the same-root maj↔dom part of it.
  • The other same-root confusions are untouched: min↔maj, min↔dom, and hdim↔min (the flat-five analogue, which would need the triad head rather than the seventh head).
  • Seven songs, four of which carry the confusion at all. Single-corpus finding until a broader jazz set confirms it. It is a strict no-op on purely diatonic pop.
  • The context witness reads the next chart symbol, so it inherits that symbol's root error, and it uses one symbol of lookahead — fine offline, not causal.

Default OFF. HARMONIA_FUNCTION_FAMILY=1 to enable, HARMONIA_FUNCTION_FAMILY_W to move the one knob. OFF returns the identical list object, proven in tests/test_function_family.py. Not wired into the pipeline — that touches a concurrently-owned file.

Both references are reported everywhere because the frozen reference is confirmed ~12% wrong and is a metronomic lattice. The repaired one drops 14.5% of the time selected because the model disagreed there, so it flatters us; the raw one contains labels Louis's ear rejected. The brick wins on both, by similar margins, which is the only reason to believe either number.
