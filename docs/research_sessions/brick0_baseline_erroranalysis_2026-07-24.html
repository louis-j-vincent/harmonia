9 · New hypotheses beyond the P1–P4 backlog

  • The 7th-quality axis is a separable, under-weighted lever. On the two cleanest-root songs the residual is almost entirely quality tokens, not root: close_to_you (root .864 / strict .235) and backing (root .879 / 7ths .499). 157s of loss occurs with root already correct. A 7th-degree "one-note" discriminator (P2a) would move 7ths/strict on exactly the songs where the root/fifth problem is already solved — arguably readier than the root fight there.
  • brick0 GT has a small mislabeled tail. iReal+model both vote against brick0 on ~18s (bein_green ~115–130s the clearest). A targeted GT re-verify of those spans would slightly lift the "true" ceiling and is cheap.
  • "Other" intervals (17% of root loss, 74s) are diffuse note-detection noise (M2/m2/m6/M3), not a clean theoretic target — a floor on what post-hoc root editing can recover, reinforcing P1c (better front-end) over re-adjudication.
