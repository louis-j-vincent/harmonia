Arm 4 — the reframed target: "does the block reproduce what is played?"

SALAMI letters are the wrong ground truth for a CHORD chart. A verse and a chorus over the identical loop are different letters but the same chords — writing them once loses nothing on a chord sheet. So here the target is not the letter: positive = the span-1 template, tiled, reproduces ≥95% of span 2's chord-tone TIME (GT labels); negative = <80%.
